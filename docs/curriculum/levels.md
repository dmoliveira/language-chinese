# Curriculum Levels

## Level 0: Foundations

- pinyin and tone awareness
- core pronunciation habits
- greetings and survival phrases
- first high-frequency characters and words

## Level 1: Beginner

- simple sentence patterns
- everyday topics
- core listening and reading comprehension
- short guided writing and speaking tasks

## Level 2: Early Intermediate

- longer dialogues and short texts
- more flexible sentence building
- short summaries and explanations
- topic-based study expansion

## Level 3: Functional Intermediate

- discussion of familiar themes
- longer reading passages
- practical writing and speaking with moderate independence
- stronger transfer across topics

## Technical Track Overlay

- applied vocabulary for software, product work, data, AI, and research
- designed as an optional overlay on top of general learner levels
- should appear first in Level 1 and expand through Levels 2 and 3

## Level design rules

1. learners should always have a general path before a technical specialization path
2. each level should define starter listening, reading, writing, and speaking outcomes
3. each level should map to flashcards, study plans, and milestone checks

Family and Personal Information Core Set

Review beginner family words and simple personal information patterns.

Deck Purpose

Support quick review of beginner family words and personal information phrases.

Starter card ideas

  • 妈妈 ↔ mother
  • 爸爸 ↔ father
  • 姐姐 ↔ older sister
  • 我是学生 ↔ I am a student
  • 我学习中文 ↔ I study Chinese

How to use this deck

  • recognize the family word
  • say the pinyin aloud
  • make one short sentence with it

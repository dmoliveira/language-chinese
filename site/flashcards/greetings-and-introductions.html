Greetings and Introductions Deck

Starter review cards for common greetings and self-introduction phrases.

Deck Purpose

Support quick review of the first greetings, self-introduction phrases, and polite meeting language.

Included Lessons

  • Greetings and Introductions
  • Build Simple Self-Introduction Sentences
  • Pronunciation, Tones, and Basic Self-Introduction

Card Design Notes

  • 你好 ↔ nǐ hǎo ↔ hello
  • 我叫 ... ↔ wǒ jiào ... ↔ my name is ...
  • 很高兴认识你 ↔ hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ ↔ nice to meet you

Audience

This plan is for learners who are starting Chinese from zero and want a short, structured beginning.

Time Commitment

  • 15 to 25 minutes per day
  • 6 study days per week
  • 1 lighter review or rest day per week

Weekly Plan

Week 1

  • Day 1: read Pinyin and Tone Basics
  • Day 2: study Pronunciation, Tones, and Basic Self-Introduction
  • Day 3: study Greetings and Introductions
  • Day 4: review Greetings and Introductions Deck
  • Day 5: study First Characters and High-Frequency Words
  • Day 6: self-review and speak your introduction aloud

Week 2

  • Day 1: read Pronunciation Guide
  • Day 2: study Build Simple Self-Introduction Sentences
  • Day 3: review Core Starter Vocabulary Deck
  • Day 4: repeat listening and speaking practice
  • Day 5: do Entry Self-Check
  • Day 6: do Beginner Lesson Quiz

Lesson Sequence

  • Pinyin and Tone Basics
  • Pronunciation, Tones, and Basic Self-Introduction
  • Greetings and Introductions
  • First Characters and High-Frequency Words
  • Pronunciation Guide
  • Build Simple Self-Introduction Sentences
  • Greetings and Introductions Deck
  • Core Starter Vocabulary Deck
  • Entry Self-Check
  • Beginner Lesson Quiz

Review Checkpoints

  • Can you say 你好，我叫 ... clearly?
  • Can you recognize 我, 你, and 好?
  • Can you read and understand a very short self-introduction?

Completion Criteria

You complete this plan when you can greet someone simply, say your name in Chinese, read a short beginner text with support, and complete the first self-check and lesson quiz pattern.

Best use of this page

Use this as your daily checklist. Keep the page open and move through the sequence one task at a time rather than jumping around the site.

Good habits for the 2-week path

  • Repeat short phrases aloud every day.
  • Keep one notebook page for new words and characters.
  • Return to the flashcards even on review days.

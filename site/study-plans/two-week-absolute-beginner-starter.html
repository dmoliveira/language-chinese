Two-Week Absolute Beginner Starter

A short guided plan for absolute beginners working through the first foundation lessons.

Audience

For learners starting Chinese from zero who want a short, structured beginning.

Time Commitment

  • 15 to 25 minutes per day
  • 6 study days per week
  • 1 lighter review or rest day per week

Weekly Plan

Week 1

  • Day 1: Pinyin and Tone Basics
  • Day 2: Pronunciation, Tones, and Basic Self-Introduction
  • Day 3: Greetings and Introductions
  • Day 4: Greetings and Introductions Deck
  • Day 5: First Characters and High-Frequency Words
  • Day 6: self-review and speak your introduction aloud

Week 2

  • Day 1: Pronunciation Guide
  • Day 2: Build Simple Self-Introduction Sentences
  • Day 3: Core Starter Vocabulary Deck
  • Day 4: repeat listening and speaking practice
  • Day 5: Entry Self-Check
  • Day 6: Beginner Lesson Quiz

Lesson Sequence

  • Pinyin and Tone Basics
  • Pronunciation, Tones, and Basic Self-Introduction
  • Greetings and Introductions
  • First Characters and High-Frequency Words
  • Pronunciation Guide
  • Build Simple Self-Introduction Sentences
  • Greetings and Introductions Deck
  • Core Starter Vocabulary Deck
  • Entry Self-Check
  • Beginner Lesson Quiz

Review Checkpoints

  • Can you say 你好，我叫 ... clearly?
  • Can you recognize 我, 你, and 好?
  • Can you read and understand a very short self-introduction?

Completion Criteria

You should be able to greet someone simply, say your name in Chinese, read a short beginner text with support, and complete the first self-check and quiz.

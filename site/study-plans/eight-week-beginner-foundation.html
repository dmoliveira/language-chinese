Study plan

Eight-Week Beginner Foundation

A slower and more repetitive beginner path for learners who want steady review.

Audience

For learners who prefer slower pacing, more repetition, and steady confidence-building.

Time Commitment

  • 20 to 30 minutes per study day
  • 5 study days per week
  • Optional weekend review

Weekly Plan

Weeks 1 to 2

Pinyin, tones, and pronunciation basics with Pinyin and Tone Basics, then daily speaking practice.

Weeks 3 to 4

Greetings and Introductions plus repeated listening and flashcard review.

Weeks 5 to 6

First Characters and High-Frequency Words plus Core Starter Vocabulary Deck review.

Weeks 7 to 8

Build Simple Self-Introduction Sentences plus Entry Self-Check, Beginner Lesson Quiz, and Beginner Milestone Checklist.

Lesson Sequence

  • Pinyin and Tone Basics
  • Pronunciation Guide
  • Pronunciation, Tones, and Basic Self-Introduction
  • Greetings and Introductions
  • First Characters and High-Frequency Words
  • Build Simple Self-Introduction Sentences
  • Entry Self-Check
  • Beginner Lesson Quiz
  • Beginner Milestone Checklist

Review Checkpoints

  • Can you hear and repeat key beginner phrases?
  • Can you read the first short text with support?
  • Can you write a short self-introduction?
  • Can you explain your current strengths and gaps?

Completion Criteria

You should be able to understand and produce the core starter phrases, read the first guided text with support, write a short self-introduction, and finish the beginner milestone checklist.

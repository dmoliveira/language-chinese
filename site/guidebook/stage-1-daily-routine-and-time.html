Stage 1 — Daily Routine and Time

Use routine verbs and time words across all four skills.

Target outcomes

  • can describe a simple daily routine
  • can recognize basic time words and routine verbs
  • can read and write short routine sentences
  • can speak for 20 to 30 seconds about a day schedule

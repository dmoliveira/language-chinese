Stage 2 — Food, Needs, and Preferences

Learn to understand and express food choices, wants, and simple preferences.

Target outcomes

  • can recognize common food and drink words
  • can ask or answer simple preference questions
  • can read a small menu-like text
  • can write or say what they like or want

Stage 3 — Family, People, and Personal Information

Learn to describe family members, basic personal information, and simple people-related facts.

Target outcomes

  • can talk about family members in short sentences
  • can describe a person simply
  • can give basic personal information
  • can understand short people/family descriptions

What this stage covers

  • family words and relationships
  • simple personal information patterns
  • short descriptive sentences about a person
  • identity, study, and work basics

Unit sequence

  • Unit 3 — Family, People, and Personal Information

Review support

  • Family and Personal Information Core Set
  • Introduce a Person Checkpoint

How to study this stage

  • learn the family words first
  • practice one identity pattern at a time
  • describe one person before trying many people

Stage 2 — Reasons, Choices, and Short Explanations

Learn to explain simple choices and give short reasons in connected language.

Target outcomes

  • can explain a simple preference with a reason
  • can answer why-questions in short sentences
  • can read and write short choice explanations
  • can speak in 4 to 6 connected lines about a simple decision

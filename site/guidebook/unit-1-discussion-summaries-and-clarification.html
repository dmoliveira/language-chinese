Unit 1 — Discussion, Summaries, and Clarification

Use summary, opinion, and clarification skills across all four skills.

Lessons

  • Listening to a Short Everyday Discussion
  • Reading a Short Summary Text
  • Write a Short Summary and Opinion
  • Discuss a Familiar Topic Clearly

References

  • Summary Language Basics
  • Clarification and Follow-up Questions

Suggested study order

  1. read the summary and clarification references
  2. do listening and reading first
  3. then move into writing and speaking
  4. finish with the checkpoint

Decks and checks

  • Summary and Discussion Core Set
  • Summarize and Respond Checkpoint

Expected outcome

By the end of this unit, the learner should be able to summarize a short topic and add one short opinion or clarification.

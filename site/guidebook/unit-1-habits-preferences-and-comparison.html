Handbook unit

Unit 1 — Habits, Preferences, and Comparison

Use habit words, simple comparison, and preference explanations across all four skills.

Lessons

  • Listening to Everyday Habits
  • Reading a Short Preference Comparison
  • Write About Your Habits and Preferences
  • Talk About What You Usually Do and Prefer

References

  • Habit and Frequency Basics
  • Simple Comparison Basics

Suggested study order

  1. read the habit/frequency reference
  2. do the listening and reading pages
  3. move into writing and speaking
  4. finish with the deck and checkpoint

Decks and checks

  • Habits and Preferences Core Set
  • Explain a Simple Preference Checkpoint

Expected outcome

By the end of this unit, the learner should be able to describe a simple habit and a preference in 3 to 4 connected lines.

Part I — Foundations

Build the sound system, first sentence patterns, and first beginner interactions.

Learner outcomes

  • understand pinyin and tone basics
  • produce a short self-introduction
  • read a tiny supported text
  • write a tiny supported self-introduction
  • complete a first self-check and guided study plan

Stage sequence

  • Stage 1 — Sound and First Introductions

Use this part as your full beginner foundation block before moving deeper into everyday Chinese.

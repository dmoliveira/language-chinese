Stage 4 — Places, Movement, and Basic Plans

Learn to talk about where things are, where you go, and simple future plans.

Target outcomes

  • can use basic place words such as here and there
  • can use simple movement verbs such as go and come
  • can describe a simple plan with time and place
  • can read and write short place-based beginner texts

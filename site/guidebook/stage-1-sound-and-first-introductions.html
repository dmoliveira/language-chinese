Stage 1 — Sound and First Introductions

Learn the sound system, then use it in greetings, listening, reading, and writing.

Target outcomes

  • can read pinyin with basic tone awareness
  • can introduce self in a short line
  • can understand a short greeting dialogue
  • can read and write a tiny beginner text with support

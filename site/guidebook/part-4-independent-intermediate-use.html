Part IV — Independent Intermediate Use

Build more independent communication through discussion, summary, explanation, and clarification.

Learner outcomes

  • discuss familiar topics with more flexibility
  • summarize short content
  • explain reasons and opinions more clearly
  • ask for and give clarification in more natural ways

Stage sequence

  • Stage 1 — Discussion, Summaries, and Clarification

Entry note

This part works best after completing Part III — Everyday Expansion.

Best use

Use this part when you want to move from supported expansion work into more independent real-world discussion and response tasks.

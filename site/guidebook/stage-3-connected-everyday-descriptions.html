Stage 3 — Connected Everyday Descriptions

Learn to combine multiple short sentences into clearer everyday description paragraphs.

Target outcomes

  • can describe a simple person, routine, or place in connected sentences
  • can organize short information in a more natural order
  • can understand a slightly longer everyday text
  • can produce a small connected paragraph or spoken response

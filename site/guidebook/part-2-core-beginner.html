Part II — Core Beginner

Build practical everyday beginner Chinese across routine, time, food, people, places, and connected short expressions.

Learner outcomes

  • understand short routine descriptions
  • describe a simple day
  • read and write short practical texts
  • speak in short connected beginner sentences
  • handle simple food and preference language
  • talk about people, family, places, and simple plans

Stage sequence

  • Stage 1 — Daily Routine and Time
  • Stage 2 — Food, Needs, and Preferences
  • Stage 3 — Family, People, and Personal Information
  • Stage 4 — Places, Movement, and Basic Plans

Entry note

This part works best after completing Part I — Foundations.

Stage 4 — Opinions and Clarifications

Learn to express a simple opinion and clarify meaning in short connected language.

Target outcomes

  • can say a simple opinion clearly
  • can ask for or give a simple clarification
  • can read short texts with opinion language
  • can write and say a short supported opinion paragraph

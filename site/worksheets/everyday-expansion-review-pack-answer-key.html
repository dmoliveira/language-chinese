Everyday Expansion Review Pack — Answer Key

Suggested answers for the Part III everyday expansion review pack.

Habits & Preferences Review

Accept 3 lines that include a habit, a frequency word, and one preference or comparison, for example: 我每天喝茶。 我常常看书。 我比他更喜欢茶。

Reasons & Choices Review

Accept one short reason-response pattern, for example: 因为我喜欢茶，所以我常常喝茶。

Connected Description Review

Accept a 4-line paragraph with a clear topic and at least one connector, for example: 这是我的一天。 我早上七点起床。 然后我去学校。 晚上我看书。

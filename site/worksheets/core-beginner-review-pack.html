Printable review

Core Beginner Review Pack

A print-friendly pack for reviewing the Level 1 beginner material.

Routine Review

  • Write 3 lines about your daily routine.
  • Circle all time words.
  • Underline all routine verbs.

Food & Preferences Review

  • Write one sentence with 喜欢, one with 想, and one with 要.
  • Add one yes/no food question using 吗.

Family Review

  • Write 3 lines introducing one family member.
  • Add one work/study detail.

Places & Plans Review

  • Write one plan with 明天.
  • Write one location sentence with 在 + place.
  • Say both aloud after writing.

Checkpoint Prompt

Can you describe your routine, preferences, a person, and a simple plan in short connected sentences?

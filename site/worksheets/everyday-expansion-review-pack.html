Everyday Expansion Review Pack

A print-friendly pack for reviewing Part III everyday expansion content.

Habits & Preferences Review

  • Write 3 lines about what you usually do.
  • Add one comparison sentence.
  • Circle the frequency word in each sentence.

Reasons & Choices Review

  • Write one sentence with 因为.
  • Write one sentence with 所以.
  • Combine them into one short reason-response pattern.

Connected Description Review

  • Write a 4-line paragraph about a person, day, or place.
  • Use at least one connector such as 然后 or 也.
  • Underline the topic sentence.

Checkpoint Prompt

Can you write or say a short connected paragraph that includes a habit, a reason, or a comparison clearly?

Independent Intermediate Review Pack

A print-friendly pack for reviewing Part IV summary, discussion, and clarification skills.

Summary Review

  • Read a short paragraph and write a one-sentence summary.
  • Add one supporting detail in a second sentence.

Opinion Review

  • Write one short opinion about the summary topic.
  • Add one reason or supporting idea.

Clarification Review

  • Write two clarification questions you could ask if the topic was unclear.
  • Then answer one of them in your own words.

Checkpoint Prompt

Can you summarize a short topic, add one response, and ask one good clarification question?

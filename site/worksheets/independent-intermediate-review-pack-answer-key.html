Independent Intermediate Review Pack — Answer Key

Suggested answers for the Part IV review pack.

Summary Review

Accept one sentence stating the topic and one support sentence. Example: 这篇文章说学习中文的方法。 要常常复习。

Opinion Review

Accept one short opinion with one support detail, for example: 我觉得这个方法很好，因为很清楚。

Clarification Review

Accept questions such as: 什么意思？ / 你可以解释一下吗？ / 你是说这个方法吗？ and one short clarifying answer.

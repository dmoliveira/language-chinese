Likes and Preferences Basics

A beginner guide to saying what you like, want, or prefer.

Key Concepts

  • 喜欢 = like
  • 要 = want
  • 想 = would like / want to

Examples

我喜欢茶。
wǒ xǐhuan chá.
I like tea.
我想喝咖啡。
wǒ xiǎng hē kāfēi.
I would like to drink coffee.

Common mistakes

  • mixing 喜欢 and 要 without context
  • making the sentence longer than necessary

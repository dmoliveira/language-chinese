Starter Vocabulary Reference

A compact reference for the highest-value beginner words in the first learning slice.

Core starter words

  • 你好 — nǐ hǎo — hello
  • 我 — wǒ — I, me
  • 你 — nǐ — you
  • 好 — hǎo — good
  • 叫 — jiào — to be called
  • 名字 — míngzi — name
  • 很 — hěn — very
  • 高兴 — gāoxìng — happy, glad
  • 认识 — rènshi — to know / meet

Useful beginner chunks

  • 我叫 ... — my name is ...
  • 你呢？— and you?
  • 很高兴认识你 — nice to meet you

Examples

我叫 Lin。
wǒ jiào Lin.
My name is Lin.
很高兴认识你。
hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.
Nice to meet you.

How to study

  • learn the sound first
  • connect hanzi and pinyin second
  • use the word in a short sentence third

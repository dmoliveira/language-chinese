Reason Patterns Basics

A simple guide to expressing a short reason in everyday Chinese.

Key Concepts

  • 因为 = because
  • 所以 = so / therefore
  • keep beginner reasons short and direct

Example

因为我喜欢茶，所以我常常喝茶。
yīnwèi wǒ xǐhuan chá, suǒyǐ wǒ chángcháng hē chá.
Because I like tea, I often drink tea.

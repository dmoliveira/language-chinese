What this reference is for

Use this guide when you want a practical reminder for beginner Mandarin pronunciation during speaking and listening practice.

The goal is not perfect accent. The goal is clear, stable, understandable beginner speech.

Key concepts

  • Aim for clarity before speed.
  • Keep syllables distinct.
  • Practice common chunks, not only isolated words.

Good beginner chunks

  • 你好 — nǐ hǎo — hello
  • 我叫 ... — wǒ jiào ... — my name is ...
  • 很高兴认识你 — hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ — nice to meet you

Examples

你好
nǐ hǎo
Say both syllables clearly and evenly.
我叫 ...
wǒ jiào ...
Keep the phrase together as one speaking unit.
很高兴认识你
hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ
Keep the ending audible and do not swallow the last syllables.

Common mistakes

  • Blending all syllables into one unclear sound.
  • Dropping the final part of a word too early.
  • Copying English stress patterns instead of Mandarin syllable rhythm.
  • Trying to speak fast before the phrase is stable.

Quick speaking reminders

  • Say each chunk alone first.
  • Then combine chunks into one short line.
  • Pause naturally instead of rushing.

What success looks like

A beginner succeeds here when another learner can understand the greeting, name introduction, and polite closing without too much guessing.

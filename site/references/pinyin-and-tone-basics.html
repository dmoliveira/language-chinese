Pinyin and Tone Basics

A beginner-friendly introduction to pinyin initials, finals, and tones.

What this reference is for

Use this page to understand how beginner Chinese sounds are written in pinyin and how tones change meaning.

Key concepts

  • Pinyin is a romanization system for Mandarin Chinese.
  • A syllable often has an initial, a final, and a tone.
  • Mandarin has four main tones and one neutral tone.

Quick tone guide

  • first tone: high and level
  • second tone: rising
  • third tone: low or dipping
  • fourth tone: falling
  • neutral tone: light and short

Example: mā / má / mǎ / mà / ma

These sound different because the tone changes.

Examples

  • nǐ hǎo — hello
  • wǒ jiào — my name is
  • míngzi — name

Common mistakes

  • Ignoring tones completely.
  • Reading pinyin with English pronunciation rules.
  • Speaking too fast before the syllables are clear.

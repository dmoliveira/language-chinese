Habit and Frequency Basics

A simple guide to words for usual actions and common frequency language.

Key Concepts

  • 常常 = often
  • 有时候 = sometimes
  • 每天 = every day
  • habits are often described with short repeated-action sentences

How to use these words

  • use 每天 for a regular daily action
  • use 常常 when something happens often but not always
  • use 有时候 when the action is only occasional

Examples

我每天学习中文。
wǒ měitiān xuéxí zhōngwén.
I study Chinese every day.
我常常看书。
wǒ chángcháng kànshū.
I often read books.
我有时候喝咖啡。
wǒ yǒushíhou hē kāfēi.
I sometimes drink coffee.

Study tip

Say the frequency word first, then pair it with one familiar action. This makes habit language easier to remember and reuse.

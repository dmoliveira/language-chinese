Basic Plan and Time Expressions

A beginner guide to saying when and where something will happen.

Key Concepts

  • 今天 = today
  • 明天 = tomorrow
  • 去 = go
  • 在 + place = at / in a place

Example

我明天去学校。
wǒ míngtiān qù xuéxiào.
I am going to school tomorrow.

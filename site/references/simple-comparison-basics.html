Simple Comparison Basics

A beginner-friendly introduction to short comparison language.

Key Concepts

  • 更 = more
  • 比 = compared with
  • simple comparison should stay short at first

Example

我比他更喜欢茶。
wǒ bǐ tā gèng xǐhuan chá.
I like tea more than he does.

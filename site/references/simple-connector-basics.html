Simple Connector Basics

A beginner-friendly guide to linking two short ideas more smoothly.

Key Concepts

  • 和 = and / with
  • 但是 = but
  • 所以 = so

Example

我喜欢茶，但是他喜欢咖啡。
wǒ xǐhuan chá, dànshì tā xǐhuan kāfēi.
I like tea, but he likes coffee.

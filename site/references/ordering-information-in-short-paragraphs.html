Ordering Information in Short Paragraphs

A guide to organizing short Chinese descriptions so they feel more connected and easier to follow.

Key Concepts

  • say the topic first
  • add one or two supporting facts
  • keep short descriptions in a stable order

Example

这是我的一天。我早上七点起床。白天我学习。晚上我看书。
zhè shì wǒ de yìtiān. wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng. báitiān wǒ xuéxí. wǎnshang wǒ kànshū.
This is my day. I get up at seven in the morning. During the day I study. In the evening I read.

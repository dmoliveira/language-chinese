Place Words and Location Basics

A beginner guide to here, there, and simple place/location language.

Key Concepts

  • 这里 = here
  • 那里 = there
  • 在 = to be at / in

How to use these words

  • use 这里 for here
  • use 那里 for there
  • use 在 to place a person or thing somewhere

Example

我在这里。
wǒ zài zhèlǐ.
I am here.
他在那里。
tā zài nàlǐ.
He is there.

Common mistakes

  • confusing 在 with movement verbs
  • using here/there without naming the place clearly enough

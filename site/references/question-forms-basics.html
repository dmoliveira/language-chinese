Question Forms Basics

A beginner guide to asking simple questions in Chinese.

Key Concepts

  • Yes/no questions often use 吗.
  • What questions often use 什么.
  • Simple choice questions can use 要不要.

Question types

  • 吗 questions: 你好吗？
  • 什么 questions: 你吃什么？
  • choice/want questions: 你要不要茶？

Examples

你好吗？
nǐ hǎo ma?
How are you?
你要咖啡吗？
nǐ yào kāfēi ma?
Do you want coffee?
你吃什么？
nǐ chī shénme?
What do you eat / What are you eating?

Common mistakes

  • translating English word by word
  • forgetting the question particle
  • trying to use long English-style question structure

Use this when

You need to ask for information, make a simple food choice, or check what another person wants.

Opinion Words Basics

A beginner-friendly guide to simple words for opinions and reactions.

Key Concepts

  • 觉得 = think / feel
  • 很好 = very good
  • 不错 = not bad

Example

我觉得这个很好。
wǒ juéde zhège hěn hǎo.
I think this is very good.

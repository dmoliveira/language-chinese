Clarification and Follow-up Questions

A practical guide to asking for more detail, meaning, and confirmation.

Key Concepts

  • 什么意思？= what does it mean?
  • 你可以解释一下吗？= can you explain a bit?
  • 你是说...吗？= do you mean ...?

When to use these questions

  • when you miss one important word
  • when the meaning is not clear
  • when you want to confirm you understood correctly

Examples

什么意思？
shénme yìsi?
What does it mean?
你可以解释一下吗？
nǐ kěyǐ jiěshì yíxià ma?
Can you explain a bit?
你是说这个方法吗？
nǐ shì shuō zhège fāngfǎ ma?
Do you mean this method?

Study tip

These questions are practical survival tools. Even intermediate learners use them often in real conversation.

Good habit

If you do not understand one word, ask for clarification immediately instead of pretending you understood the whole sentence.

Summary Language Basics

A practical guide to the first useful words and patterns for simple summaries.

Key Concepts

  • first say the topic
  • then give 1 to 2 important details
  • keep the summary shorter than the original text

Example

这篇文章说学习中文的方法。
zhè piān wénzhāng shuō xuéxí zhōngwén de fāngfǎ.
This text talks about ways to study Chinese.

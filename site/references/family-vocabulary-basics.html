Family Vocabulary Basics

A beginner guide to the most useful family words and simple family-description patterns.

Key words

  • 家人 — family
  • 妈妈 — mother
  • 爸爸 — father
  • 哥哥 — older brother
  • 姐姐 — older sister
  • 弟弟 — younger brother
  • 妹妹 — younger sister

How to use these words

At this level, you do not need long family stories. Start with naming one person and one fact about them.

  • 这是我妈妈。— This is my mother.
  • 我爸爸是老师。— My father is a teacher.
  • 我姐姐学习中文。— My older sister studies Chinese.

Example

这是我妈妈。
zhè shì wǒ māma.
This is my mother.

Common mistakes

  • using too many family words before the first few feel stable
  • trying to describe the whole family at once

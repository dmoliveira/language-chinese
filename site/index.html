Language Chinese

An open Chinese-learning project for English speakers, with strong support for technical and AI-related learning goals.

This site is designed to grow into a public, accessible learning hub with lessons, references, study plans, flashcards, and guided evaluation pages.

What you can use now

  • starter beginner lessons across listening, reading, writing, and speaking
  • a first handbook structure with Part, Stage, and Unit pages
  • pronunciation and pinyin references
  • beginner study plans and evaluation pages
  • a public open-learning project structure ready for GitHub Pages

If you are unsure where to begin, use Start Here and follow the guided beginner path through the first reference pages, lessons, self-check, and study plan.

Project direction

The long-term goal is to support general Chinese learning while also helping learners who want Chinese for software, computer science, product work, and AI/ML contexts.

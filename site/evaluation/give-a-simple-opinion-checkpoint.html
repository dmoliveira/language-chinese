Give a Simple Opinion Checkpoint

A short checkpoint for expressing one simple opinion and one supporting idea.

Task

Write or say 4 to 6 lines giving one simple opinion and one supporting idea.

Success Criteria

  • includes an opinion phrase
  • includes one supporting reason or detail
  • communicates clearly

Explain a Simple Choice Checkpoint

A short checkpoint for explaining one simple choice with a short reason.

Task

Write or say 4 to 6 lines explaining one simple choice and one short reason.

Success Criteria

  • includes one choice statement
  • includes one reason
  • uses at least one connector or structure clearly

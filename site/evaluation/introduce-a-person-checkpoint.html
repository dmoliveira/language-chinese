Introduce a Person Checkpoint

A short checkpoint for introducing a family member or another person simply.

Task

Write or say 4 to 6 lines introducing a family member or another person.

Success Criteria

  • uses a few family or identity words
  • gives simple personal information
  • communicates clearly

Suggested structure

  • say who the person is
  • say one family or identity word
  • say one study/work detail
  • finish with one simple description sentence

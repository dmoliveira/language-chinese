Tech Self-Introduction Checkpoint

A short checkpoint for introducing your tech study or work focus in beginner Chinese.

Task

Write or say 4 to 6 lines about what you study or work on in tech.

Success Criteria

  • uses at least two tech words
  • gives one clear study/work focus
  • communicates in short understandable lines

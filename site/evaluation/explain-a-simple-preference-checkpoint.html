Explain a Simple Preference Checkpoint

A short checkpoint for describing habits and explaining one simple preference.

Task

Write or say 4 to 6 lines about what you usually do and what you prefer.

Success Criteria

  • uses one frequency or habit word
  • expresses one preference clearly
  • includes at least one connecting or comparison idea

Suggested structure

  • say one thing you usually do
  • say one thing you often or sometimes do
  • say what you prefer
  • if ready, compare two options

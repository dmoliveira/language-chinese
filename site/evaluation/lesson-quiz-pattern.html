Beginner Lesson Quiz

A concrete beginner quiz for checking understanding after the first foundation lessons.

Purpose

Use this page to check understanding after the first foundation lessons.

Instructions

Answer each question without looking first. Then compare with the sample answers and guidance below.

Try one round without hints, then a second round after review.

Questions or Tasks

  1. Recognition: what does 你好 mean?
  2. Matching: connect 我叫 ... with “my name is ...”
  3. Reading: identify the word 名字 in a short sentence
  4. Production: write or say a one-line self-introduction

Scoring or Interpretation Guidance

  • 4 strong responses: ready to move on
  • 2 to 3 strong responses: review once more
  • 0 to 1 strong responses: repeat the lesson and reference pages

Sample answers and rubric:
1. 你好 means hello.
2. 我叫 ... means my name is ....
3. 名字 means name.
4. A good answer is a short correct sentence such as 你好，我叫 Alex。

Use the first attempt as a diagnostic check, not as a final grade. The purpose of this page is to tell you what to review next.

What this quiz checks

  • basic meaning recognition
  • starter phrase recall
  • very short reading comprehension
  • simple output ability

How to use your result

  • Strong result: move to the study plan and continue practicing.
  • Mixed result: revisit one lesson and one deck.
  • Weak result: restart at Start Here and repeat the first reference pages.

Recommended Next Steps

  • Review the lesson once more if needed
  • Revisit the related reference page
  • Use the related flashcard deck before trying again
  • If you want a guided sequence, continue with Two-Week Absolute Beginner Starter

Build a Short Everyday Description Checkpoint

A short checkpoint for producing a small connected everyday description.

Task

Write or say 4 to 6 connected lines describing a routine, person, or place.

Success Criteria

  • includes a clear topic
  • includes supporting details
  • uses at least one connector or linking cue

Summarize and Respond Checkpoint

A short checkpoint for summarizing a small topic and giving a response.

Task

Summarize a short topic and give one opinion or response in 4 to 6 lines.

Success Criteria

  • identifies the main idea
  • gives one or two supporting details
  • adds a short response clearly

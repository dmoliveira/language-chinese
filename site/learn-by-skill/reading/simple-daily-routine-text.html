Level 1 reading

Simple Daily Routine Reading

Read a short text about a beginner daily routine and understand the order of actions.

Text

我早上七点起床。 我吃早饭。 白天我学习。 晚上我休息。
wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng. wǒ chī zǎofàn. báitiān wǒ xuéxí. wǎnshang wǒ xiūxi.
I get up at seven in the morning. I eat breakfast. I study during the day. I rest in the evening.

Practice

  1. What time does the person get up?
  2. Which word means evening?
  3. What does the person do during the day?

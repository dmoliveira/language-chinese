Reading a Short Summary Text

Read a short summary and identify the main idea and supporting points.

Objectives

  • read a short summary text
  • identify the main point and support details

Reading text

这篇文章说学习中文的方法。第一，要常常复习。第二，要多听多说。
zhè piān wénzhāng shuō xuéxí zhōngwén de fāngfǎ. dì-yī, yào chángcháng fùxí. dì-èr, yào duō tīng duō shuō.
This text talks about ways to study Chinese. First, review often. Second, listen and speak more.

What to notice

  • the first sentence states the topic
  • the following sentences give supporting points
  • summary writing often uses ordered support like first / second

Practice

  1. What is the topic of the text?
  2. What is the first method?
  3. What is the second method?
  4. Which sentence is the summary sentence?

Answer Key

  1. Ways to study Chinese.
  2. Review often.
  3. Listen and speak more.
  4. 这篇文章说学习中文的方法。

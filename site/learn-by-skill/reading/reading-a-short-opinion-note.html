Reading a Short Opinion Note

Read a short note that expresses an opinion and one support detail.

Objectives

  • read a short opinion note
  • identify the opinion and one supporting detail

Reading text

我觉得这个很好，因为很方便。
wǒ juéde zhège hěn hǎo, yīnwèi hěn fāngbiàn.
I think this is very good because it is convenient.

What to notice

  • the opinion comes first
  • the reason follows after 因为
  • the whole note is still short and clear

Practice

  1. What does the writer think?
  2. What is the reason?
  3. Which word introduces the reason?
  4. Can you rewrite the note in your own English words?

Answer Key

  1. The writer thinks it is very good.
  2. Because it is convenient.
  3. 因为
  4. Any accurate short paraphrase is acceptable.

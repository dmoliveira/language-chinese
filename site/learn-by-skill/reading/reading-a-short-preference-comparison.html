Level 2 reading

Reading a Short Preference Comparison

Read a short text comparing two habits or preferences.

Objectives

  • read a short connected comparison text
  • identify one preference and one comparison point

Reading text

我每天喝茶。他常常喝咖啡。我比他更喜欢茶。
wǒ měitiān hē chá. tā chángcháng hē kāfēi. wǒ bǐ tā gèng xǐhuan chá.
I drink tea every day. He often drinks coffee. I like tea more than he does.

What to notice

  • the first two sentences describe habits
  • the last sentence introduces comparison
  • short connected text is enough at this level

Practice

  1. What does the first person drink every day?
  2. What does the second person often drink?
  3. Which line shows comparison?
  4. Find one frequency word.

Answer Key

  1. Tea.
  2. Coffee.
  3. 我比他更喜欢茶。
  4. 每天 or 常常

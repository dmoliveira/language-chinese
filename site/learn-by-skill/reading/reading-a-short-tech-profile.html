Reading a Short Tech Profile

Read a short profile about someone learning coding, software, or AI.

Objectives

  • read a short tech learner profile
  • identify what the person studies or works on

Reading text

我叫 Lin。我学习人工智能，也写代码。
wǒ jiào Lin. wǒ xuéxí réngōng zhìnéng, yě xiě dàimǎ.
My name is Lin. I study artificial intelligence and also write code.

What to notice

  • the profile still uses beginner self-introduction patterns
  • one tech field and one activity are enough for a useful short profile
  • the word 也 helps connect two related ideas

Practice

  1. What is the person’s name?
  2. What does the person study?
  3. What does the person also do?
  4. Which connector means “also”?

Answer Key

  1. Lin.
  2. Artificial intelligence.
  3. Writes code.
  4. 也

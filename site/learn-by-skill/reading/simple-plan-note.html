Reading a Simple Plan Note

Read a short note about where and when something will happen.

Objectives

  • read a short note about place and time
  • identify basic plan details

Reading text

我明天去学校。下午我在图书馆。
wǒ míngtiān qù xuéxiào. xiàwǔ wǒ zài túshūguǎn.
I am going to school tomorrow. In the afternoon I am at the library.

What to notice

  • 明天 gives the time
  • 去 gives movement
  • 在图书馆 gives the location

Practice

  1. When does the person go to school?
  2. Where is the person in the afternoon?
  3. Which word means go?
  4. Which word means library?

Answer Key

  1. Tomorrow.
  2. At the library.
  3. 去
  4. 图书馆

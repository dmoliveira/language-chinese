Reading lesson

First Characters and High-Frequency Words

Read a few essential beginner characters and words in a short guided text.

Objectives

  • Recognize a small set of common beginner characters.
  • Match hanzi with pinyin and English meaning.
  • Read a very short beginner text with support.

Prerequisites

  • No prior reading knowledge required.
  • Helpful: basic pinyin familiarity.

Vocabulary and Patterns

  • 我 — wǒ — I, me
  • 你 — nǐ — you
  • 好 — hǎo — good
  • 叫 — jiào — to be called
  • 名字 — míngzi — name

Short combinations: 你好 — nǐ hǎo — hello; 我叫 ... — wǒ jiào ... — my name is ...

Lesson Content

Read this short text three times:

你好！(nǐ hǎo) — Hello!

我叫 Lin。(wǒ jiào Lin.) — My name is Lin.

你叫什么名字？(nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?) — What is your name?

Reading support

  • 你好 = hello
  • 我叫 Lin = My name is Lin
  • 你叫什么名字？ = What is your name?

What to notice

  • 我 appears in self-introductions.
  • 你 appears when speaking directly to another person.
  • 叫 is useful very early because it helps you say your name.

Practice

  1. Which character means “I”?
  2. Which character means “you”?
  3. Find the phrase that asks for a name.
  4. Read the short text aloud once with pinyin support.

Answer Key or Rubric

  1. 我
  2. 你
  3. 你叫什么名字？
  4. Successful if the learner can roughly map each line to meaning and pronunciation.

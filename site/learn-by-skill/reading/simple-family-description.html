Level 1 reading

Reading a Simple Family Description

Read a short text that describes a family member or another person simply.

Objectives

  • read a short person or family description
  • identify simple personal information

Reading text

这是我姐姐。她是学生。她学习中文。
zhè shì wǒ jiějie. tā shì xuésheng. tā xuéxí zhōngwén.
This is my older sister. She is a student. She studies Chinese.

What to notice

  • short identity sentences are enough at this level
  • one family word + one role + one activity makes a useful paragraph

Practice

  1. Who is being described?
  2. What is her role?
  3. What does she study?
  4. Underline the family word.

Answer Key

  1. The older sister.
  2. She is a student.
  3. Chinese.
  4. 姐姐

Level 1 listening

Family Introduction Listening

Listen for simple family words and personal information in a short introduction.

Objectives

  • recognize simple family words
  • understand a short introduction to a person

Before you listen

  • Look for family words first.
  • Then listen for who the person is.
  • Finally listen for one extra personal detail.

Vocabulary focus

  • 妈妈 — mother
  • 爸爸 — father
  • 姐姐 — older sister
  • 学生 — student
  • 老师 — teacher

Sample listening lines

这是我姐姐。
zhè shì wǒ jiějie.
This is my older sister.
她是学生。
tā shì xuésheng.
She is a student.
我爸爸是老师。
wǒ bàba shì lǎoshī.
My father is a teacher.

Practice

  1. Which family word do you hear first?
  2. Which line gives a job or role?
  3. Who is the student?
  4. Listen again and identify one family word and one identity word.

Answer Key

  1. 姐姐 in the example order above.
  2. 她是学生。 / 我爸爸是老师。
  3. The older sister.
  4. Any correct family word + identity word pair is acceptable.

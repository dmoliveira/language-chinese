Level 1 listening

Simple Daily Routine Listening

Listen for time words, routine verbs, and short daily-life statements.

Objectives

  • Understand a short daily routine description.
  • Recognize common time words and routine verbs.
  • Identify the order of actions in a simple day.

Sample mini-text

我早上七点起床。
wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng.
I get up at seven in the morning.
白天我学习。
báitiān wǒ xuéxí.
I study during the day.

Practice

  1. What happens first?
  2. Which word means morning?
  3. Which line is about studying?

Greetings and Introductions

Learn to recognize and respond to simple beginner greetings.

Where you are in the beginner path

You have moved from pronunciation into your first listening lesson. After this, continue to the first reading lesson.

Objectives

  • Recognize a few common greetings in pinyin and hanzi.
  • Understand a short self-introduction.
  • Reply with a simple greeting and name.

Vocabulary and Patterns

  • 你好 — nǐ hǎo — hello
  • 你好吗？— nǐ hǎo ma? — how are you?
  • 我很好 — wǒ hěn hǎo — I am very well
  • 我叫 Anna — wǒ jiào Anna — my name is Anna
  • 很高兴认识你 — hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ — nice to meet you

Pattern: 我叫 + name

Useful response chunks: 你好！ / 你呢？ / 很高兴认识你。

Lesson Content

Listen for the overall meaning first. Do not worry if you cannot catch every syllable on the first try.

A: 你好！
nǐ hǎo
Hello!
B: 我叫 Anna。你呢？
wǒ jiào Anna. nǐ ne?
My name is Anna. And you?
A: 我叫 David。很高兴认识你。
wǒ jiào David. hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.
My name is David. Nice to meet you.

Listening tips

  • Notice that nǐ hǎo is short and clear.
  • Listen for wǒ jiào to catch a name introduction.
  • Listen for nǐ ne to hear “and you?”
  • Notice the polite closing at the end.

Shadowing routine

  1. Repeat only 你好 and 我叫 ...
  2. Repeat full short lines.
  3. Say the whole dialogue with pauses.

Practice

  1. What phrase means “my name is ...”?
  2. In the sample dialogue, what are the two names?
  3. Which line means “nice to meet you”?
  4. Say your own name after: 我叫 ...
  5. Which phrase means “and you?”
  6. Listen again and count how many times 你好 appears.

Answer Key or Rubric

  1. 我叫 ...
  2. Anna and David
  3. 很高兴认识你
  4. Any clear self-introduction is acceptable.
  5. 你呢？
  6. Two times.

Before You Listen

  • You do not need to understand every word.
  • Your first goal is to catch greeting, name, and polite closing.
  • Your second goal is to hear repeated chunks.

  • Change both names in the dialogue.
  • Say the dialogue faster but keep the words clear.
  • Cover the English meaning and use only hanzi or pinyin.
  • Listen once, then summarize the dialogue in English.

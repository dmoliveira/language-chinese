Level 2 listening

Listening to Everyday Habits

Listen to a short description of what someone usually does and prefers.

Objectives

  • understand a short connected habit description
  • notice one simple preference or comparison

Before you listen

  • Listen first for frequency words such as every day or often.
  • Then listen for what the speaker usually does.
  • Finally listen for one preference or comparison point.

Vocabulary focus

  • 每天 — every day
  • 常常 — often
  • 有时候 — sometimes
  • 喜欢 — like
  • 更 — more

Sample listening lines

我每天早上喝茶。
wǒ měitiān zǎoshang hē chá.
I drink tea every morning.
我常常看书。
wǒ chángcháng kànshū.
I often read books.
我比他更喜欢茶。
wǒ bǐ tā gèng xǐhuan chá.
I like tea more than he does.

Practice

  1. Which frequency word appears in the first line?
  2. What does the speaker often do?
  3. Which line shows a comparison?
  4. Listen again and identify one habit and one preference.

Answer Key

  1. 每天
  2. Reads books.
  3. 我比他更喜欢茶。
  4. Any correct pair such as drinks tea + likes tea more.

Level 1 writing

Write Your Basic Daily Routine

Write 3 to 4 simple sentences about your day using time words and routine verbs.

Pattern support

我早上七点起床。
wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng.
I get up at seven in the morning.
我晚上学习。
wǒ wǎnshang xuéxí.
I study in the evening.

Practice

Write 3 to 4 sentences about your day.

Rubric

  • includes time words
  • includes routine verbs
  • sentence order is understandable

Write Simple Food Preferences

Write a few short sentences about what you like, want, or choose to eat and drink.

Objectives

  • write short food preference sentences
  • use 喜欢 / 要 / 想 in simple patterns

Practice

Write 3 to 4 short food preference sentences.

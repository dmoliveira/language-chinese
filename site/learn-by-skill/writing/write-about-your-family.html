Level 1 writing

Write About Your Family

Write a few short sentences to introduce a family member or another person.

Objectives

  • write short family description sentences
  • include one or two personal information details

Pattern support

这是我姐姐。
zhè shì wǒ jiějie.
This is my older sister.
她是学生。
tā shì xuésheng.
She is a student.
她学习中文。
tā xuéxí zhōngwén.
She studies Chinese.

How to build the paragraph

  1. name the person
  2. say the family relationship
  3. add one identity detail
  4. add one activity or study/work detail

Practice

  1. Write one sentence naming a family member.
  2. Write one sentence giving a role or identity.
  3. Write one sentence about study or work.
  4. Combine them into a 3-line mini paragraph.
  5. Read the paragraph aloud after writing it.

Answer Key or Rubric

Possible model

  • 这是我姐姐。
  • 她是学生。
  • 她学习中文。

Rubric

  • includes a family word
  • includes one personal information detail
  • sentence order is understandable

Related references

  • Family Vocabulary Basics
  • Personal Information Patterns

Writing tip

At this level, a short clear paragraph is better than a long paragraph with unstable grammar.

Practice beginner pronunciation and say a short self-introduction clearly.

Objectives

  • Practice the sound and rhythm of a few beginner phrases.
  • Notice tone differences in common words.
  • Say a short self-introduction with confidence.

Vocabulary and Patterns

  • 你好 — nǐ hǎo
  • 我叫 ... — wǒ jiào ...
  • 很高兴认识你 — hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ

Tone reminder: nǐ = third tone, hǎo = third tone, jiào = fourth tone

Useful speaking chunks: 你好 / 我叫 ... / 很高兴认识你

Lesson Content

Practice in three steps, then turn the chunks into one full introduction.

你好，我叫 Sam。
nǐ hǎo, wǒ jiào Sam.
Hello, my name is Sam.
很高兴认识你。
hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.
Nice to meet you.

Speaking tips

  • Keep your voice steady first.
  • Focus on clear syllables before trying to sound fast.
  • Pause naturally after commas and full stops.
  • Keep 我叫 together as one chunk.

Practice

  1. Say 你好 three times slowly.
  2. Say 我叫 ... and add your own name.
  3. Record or rehearse a full line: 你好，我叫 ...。很高兴认识你。
  4. Say the line once slowly and once at natural speed.
  5. Practice with two different names.

Answer Key or Rubric

Success looks like:

  • phrases are understandable
  • learner keeps the correct word order
  • learner can complete a one-line self-introduction
  • learner keeps the introduction in one connected flow

Before You Speak

  • Say each short chunk alone.
  • Keep syllables clear.
  • Do not try to sound fast too early.

Guided Speaking Routine

  1. say each chunk alone
  2. join two chunks
  3. say the full line naturally
  4. repeat with your own name

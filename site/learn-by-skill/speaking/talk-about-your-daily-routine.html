Level 1 speaking

Talk About Your Daily Routine

Say 3 to 4 simple sentences about your day using beginner routine patterns.

Model

我早上七点起床。
wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng.
I get up at seven in the morning.
晚上我学习中文。
wǎnshang wǒ xuéxí zhōngwén.
I study Chinese in the evening.

Practice

Say 3 to 4 sentences about your day.

Success looks like

  • understandable order
  • clear short phrases
  • stable beginner rhythm

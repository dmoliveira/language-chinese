Talk About What You Study in Tech

Speak in short sentences about coding, software, or AI study/work interests.

Objectives

  • say what you study or work on in tech
  • use short beginner-friendly technical self-introduction patterns

Useful speaking chunks

  • 我学习人工智能。
  • 我写代码。
  • 我喜欢软件开发。

Speaking routine

  1. say what you study
  2. say what you do
  3. say what you like
  4. repeat the full intro more naturally

Practice

Say 3 to 4 short lines about your tech study or work interests.

Related references

  • Tech Study Vocabulary Basics
  • Simple Tech Self-Introduction Patterns

Success looks like

  • uses at least two tech words
  • keeps the intro short and clear
  • sounds like a real beginner self-introduction

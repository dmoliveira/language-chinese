Level 2 speaking

Talk About What You Usually Do and Prefer

Speak in short connected lines about habits, preferences, and one comparison.

Objectives

  • describe one or two habits
  • say one preference clearly

Useful speaking chunks

  • 我每天喝茶。
  • 我常常看书。
  • 我更喜欢茶。
  • 我比他更喜欢茶。

Speaking routine

  1. say one habit
  2. say one preference
  3. add one comparison if ready
  4. repeat the full set more smoothly

Practice

Speak 3 to 4 short connected lines about what you usually do and what you prefer.

Related references

  • Habit and Frequency Basics
  • Simple Comparison Basics

Success looks like

  • one clear habit line
  • one clear preference line
  • a short connected flow

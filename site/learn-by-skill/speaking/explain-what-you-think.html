Level 2 speaking

Explain What You Think

Speak in short connected lines to express one simple opinion clearly.

Objectives

  • express one simple opinion
  • support it with one short detail

Useful speaking chunks

  • 我觉得这个很好。
  • 我觉得那个不错。
  • 因为很方便。
  • 可以再说一次吗？

Speaking routine

  1. state your opinion
  2. add one reason
  3. repeat it more naturally
  4. ask for clarification if needed

Practice

Speak 3 to 4 short connected lines expressing one opinion and one reason.

Related references

  • Opinion Words Basics
  • Clarification Questions Basics

Success looks like

  • clear opinion line
  • one understandable supporting detail
  • calm, short spoken delivery

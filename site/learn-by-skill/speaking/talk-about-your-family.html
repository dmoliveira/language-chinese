Level 1 speaking

Talk About Your Family

Speak in short sentences to introduce a family member or another person.

Objectives

  • say who a person is
  • give one or two simple details

Useful speaking chunks

  • 这是我妈妈。
  • 我爸爸是老师。
  • 她是学生。
  • 他学习中文。

Speaking routine

  1. name the person
  2. say the relationship
  3. add one role or study/work detail
  4. say the whole introduction again smoothly

Practice

Say 3 to 4 short lines about a family member or another person.

Related references

  • Family Vocabulary Basics
  • Personal Information Patterns

Success looks like

  • clear short lines
  • one understandable identity detail
  • natural order of ideas

Level 3 speaking

Discuss a Familiar Topic Clearly

Speak in a few connected lines to summarize and respond to a familiar topic.

Objectives

  • summarize a familiar topic in short connected lines
  • add one clear response or opinion

Useful speaking chunks

  • 这篇文章说...
  • 我觉得这个方法很好。
  • 你可以再解释一下吗？

Speaking routine

  1. state the topic
  2. give one supporting detail
  3. add your response
  4. ask one clarification question if needed

Practice

Speak 3 to 4 connected lines that summarize a familiar topic and include a short response.

Related references

  • Summary Language Basics
  • Clarification and Follow-up Questions

Success looks like

  • clear topic statement
  • one support detail
  • one understandable response

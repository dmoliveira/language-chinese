Contribution guide

Contributing

Contributions should be accessible, legally safe, beginner-friendly, and appropriately scoped.

  • Use the templates under content/templates/ when contributing learning content.
  • Include metadata, attribution, and accessibility support where required.
  • Only contribute material you authored or can legally share under compatible terms.

Code contributions follow MIT. Original educational content contributions follow CC BY-SA 4.0.

# Family Vocabulary Basics

## Key words

- 家人 — family
- 妈妈 — mother
- 爸爸 — father
- 哥哥 — older brother
- 姐姐 — older sister
- 弟弟 — younger brother
- 妹妹 — younger sister

## Example

- 这是我妈妈。 — This is my mother.

# Question Forms Basics

## What This Reference Is For

Use this page to understand how simple beginner questions are built.

## Key Concepts

- Yes/no questions often use **吗**.
- “What” questions often use **什么**.
- “Do you want...?” often uses **要不要** in beginner speech.

## Examples

- 你好吗？
- 你叫什么名字？
- 你要咖啡吗？

## Common Mistakes

- translating English word by word
- forgetting the question particle

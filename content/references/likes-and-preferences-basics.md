# Likes and Preferences Basics

## What This Reference Is For

Use this page to say what you like, want, or choose in simple beginner Chinese.

## Key Concepts

- 喜欢 = like
- 要 = want
- 想 = would like / want to

## Examples

- 我喜欢茶。
- 我想喝咖啡。
- 你要米饭吗？

## Common Mistakes

- mixing 喜欢 and 要 without context
- making the sentence longer than necessary

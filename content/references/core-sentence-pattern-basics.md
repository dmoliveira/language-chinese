# Core Sentence Pattern Basics

## What This Reference Is For

Use this page to understand the first sentence shapes that appear again and again in beginner Chinese.

## Key Concepts

- Chinese beginner sentences are often short and direct.
- Word order matters more than adding many words.
- Reusing a few strong sentence frames is better than trying to invent complex grammar too early.

Starter patterns:

- 你好 — hello
- 我叫 + name — my name is ...
- 你呢？— and you?
- 很高兴认识你 — nice to meet you

## Pattern Breakdown

### Pattern 1: 我叫 + name

- 我 = I
- 叫 = am called / call
- 我叫 Anna。= My name is Anna.

### Pattern 2: 你叫什么名字？

- 你 = you
- 叫 = are called
- 什么 = what
- 名字 = name
- Natural meaning: What is your name?

### Pattern 3: 你好，我叫 ...

This combines greeting + introduction into one useful beginner sentence.

### Pattern 4: 很高兴认识你

Use this as a polite closing line after introductions.

## Examples

- 你好，我叫 Emma。
- 你叫什么名字？
- 我叫 David。你呢？
- 很高兴认识你。

## Common Mistakes

- Putting the name before **我叫**.
- Translating word by word from English and making the sentence too long.
- Forgetting that short Chinese patterns are enough at the beginner level.

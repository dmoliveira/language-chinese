# Listening to Simple Reasons

## Objectives

- understand one simple reason in connected speech
- identify one connector or reason cue

## Before You Listen

- listen first for the main choice
- then listen for the reason word or connector
- finally listen for what result or conclusion follows

## Vocabulary Focus

- 因为 — because
- 所以 — so / therefore
- 选择 — choice
- 喜欢 — like

## Sample Listening Lines

- 因为我喜欢茶，所以我常常喝茶。
- 因为他今天很忙，所以他不去学校。

## Practice

1. Which connector means “because”?
2. Which connector means “so”?
3. What does the speaker like?
4. What happens because the person is busy?

## Answer Key or Rubric

1. 因为
2. 所以
3. Tea.
4. He does not go to school.

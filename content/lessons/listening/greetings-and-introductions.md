# Greetings and Introductions

## Objectives

- Recognize a few common greetings in pinyin and hanzi.
- Understand a short self-introduction.
- Reply with a simple greeting and name.

## Prerequisites

- No prior Chinese required.
- Review `reference-foundations-001` if pinyin feels unfamiliar.

## Vocabulary and Patterns

- 你好 — nǐ hǎo — hello
- 你好吗？— nǐ hǎo ma? — how are you?
- 我很好 — wǒ hěn hǎo — I am very well
- 我叫 Anna — wǒ jiào Anna — my name is Anna
- 很高兴认识你 — hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ — nice to meet you

Pattern:

- 我叫 + name

## Lesson Content

Listen for the overall meaning first. Do not worry if you cannot catch every syllable on the first try.

Sample dialogue:

1. A: 你好！(nǐ hǎo) — Hello!
2. B: 你好！(nǐ hǎo) — Hello!
3. A: 你叫什么名字？(nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?) — What is your name?
4. B: 我叫 Anna。你呢？(wǒ jiào Anna. nǐ ne?) — My name is Anna. And you?
5. A: 我叫 David。很高兴认识你。(wǒ jiào David. hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.) — My name is David. Nice to meet you.
6. B: 很高兴认识你。(hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.) — Nice to meet you.

Listening tips:

- Notice that **nǐ hǎo** is short and clear.
- Listen for **wǒ jiào** to catch a name introduction.
- Listen for **nǐ ne** to hear “and you?”

## Practice

1. What phrase means “my name is ...”?
2. In the sample dialogue, what are the two names?
3. Which line means “nice to meet you”?
4. Say your own name after: **我叫 ...**

## Answer Key or Rubric

1. **我叫 ...**
2. **Anna** and **David**
3. **很高兴认识你**
4. Learner should produce a simple self-introduction with a name.

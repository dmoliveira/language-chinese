# Going Somewhere Listening

## Objectives

- understand a short plan about going somewhere
- identify place and time words

## Before You Listen

- Listen for where the person is going.
- Then listen for when the plan happens.
- Finally identify one location word and one movement verb.

## Vocabulary Focus

- 这里 — here
- 那里 — there
- 去 — go
- 来 — come
- 今天 — today
- 明天 — tomorrow

## Sample Listening Lines

- 我明天去学校。— I am going to school tomorrow.
- 他今天来这里。— He is coming here today.

## Practice

1. Where is the person going?
2. Which word means tomorrow?
3. Which verb means go?
4. Listen again and identify one place word and one time word.

## Answer Key or Rubric

1. School in the first example.
2. 明天
3. 去
4. Any correct pair such as 这里 + 今天 or 学校 + 明天.

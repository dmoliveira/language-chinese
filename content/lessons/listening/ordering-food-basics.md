# Ordering Food Basics Listening

## Objectives

- understand simple food choices
- recognize want/like patterns
- follow a short food-order dialogue

## Before You Listen

- Look for food words first.
- Then listen for question words and polite requests.
- Do not aim for perfect understanding on the first pass.

## Vocabulary Focus

- 咖啡 — coffee
- 茶 — tea
- 米饭 — rice
- 水 — water
- 要 — want
- 喜欢 — like

## Sample Dialogue

- A: 你要咖啡吗？— Do you want coffee?
- B: 我想喝茶。— I would like to drink tea.
- A: 你喜欢米饭吗？— Do you like rice?

## Practice

1. Which drink does speaker B want?
2. Which sentence is a yes/no question?
3. Which verb means “like”?
4. Listen again and identify one food item and one drink.

## Answer Key or Rubric

1. Tea.
2. 你要咖啡吗？ / 你喜欢米饭吗？
3. 喜欢
4. Accept any correct pair such as rice and tea.

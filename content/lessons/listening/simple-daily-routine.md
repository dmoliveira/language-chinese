# Simple Daily Routine Listening

## Objectives

- Understand a short daily routine description.
- Recognize common time words and routine verbs.
- Identify the order of actions in a simple day.

## Prerequisites

- Complete the starter listening lesson first.

## Vocabulary and Patterns

- 早上 — zǎoshang — morning
- 晚上 — wǎnshang — evening
- 起床 — qǐchuáng — get up
- 吃饭 — chīfàn — eat
- 学习 — xuéxí — study
- 工作 — gōngzuò — work

## Lesson Content

Sample mini-text:

1. 我早上七点起床。
2. 我早上吃饭。
3. 白天我学习。
4. 晚上我看书。

## Practice

1. What happens first?
2. Which word means morning?
3. Which line is about studying?

## Answer Key or Rubric

1. Getting up.
2. 早上
3. 白天我学习。

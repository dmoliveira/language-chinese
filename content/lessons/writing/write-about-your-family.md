# Write About Your Family

## Objectives

- write short family description sentences
- include a few personal information details

## Pattern Support

- 这是我姐姐。— This is my older sister.
- 她是学生。— She is a student.
- 她学习中文。— She studies Chinese.

## How To Build The Paragraph

1. name the person
2. say the family relationship
3. add one identity detail
4. add one activity or study/work detail

## Practice

1. Write one sentence naming a family member.
2. Write one sentence giving a role or identity.
3. Write one sentence about study or work.
4. Combine them into a 3-line mini paragraph.
5. Read the paragraph aloud after writing it.

## Answer Key or Rubric

Possible model:

- 这是我姐姐。
- 她是学生。
- 她学习中文。

Rubric:

- includes a family word
- includes one personal information detail
- sentence order is understandable

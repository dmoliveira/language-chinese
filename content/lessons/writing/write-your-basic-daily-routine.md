# Write Your Basic Daily Routine

## Objectives

- Write short routine sentences.
- Use time words correctly.
- Build a simple multi-sentence paragraph.

## Pattern support

- 我早上七点起床。
- 我晚上学习。

## Practice

Write 3 to 4 sentences about your day.

## Rubric

- includes time words
- includes routine verbs
- sentence order is understandable

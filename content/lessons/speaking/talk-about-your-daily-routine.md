# Talk About Your Daily Routine

## Objectives

- Say short routine sentences clearly.
- Use time words and routine verbs in order.
- Speak for 20 to 30 seconds about your day.

## Model

- 我早上七点起床。
- 我白天工作。
- 晚上我学习中文。

## Practice

Say 3 to 4 sentences about your day.

## Success looks like

- understandable order
- clear short phrases
- stable beginner rhythm

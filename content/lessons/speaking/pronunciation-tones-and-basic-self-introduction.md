# Pronunciation, Tones, and Basic Self-Introduction

## Objectives

- Practice the sound and rhythm of a few beginner phrases.
- Notice tone differences in common words.
- Say a short self-introduction with confidence.

## Prerequisites

- Recommended: read the pinyin and pronunciation references first.

## Vocabulary and Patterns

- 你好 — nǐ hǎo
- 我叫 ... — wǒ jiào ...
- 很高兴认识你 — hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ

Tone reminder:

- nǐ = third tone
- hǎo = third tone
- jiào = fourth tone

## Lesson Content

Practice in three steps:

1. Repeat each phrase slowly.
2. Say the phrase once at normal speed.
3. Join the phrases into one short introduction.

Model speaking line:

> 你好，我叫 Sam。很高兴认识你。

Speaking tips:

- Keep your voice steady first.
- Focus on clear syllables before trying to sound fast.
- Pause naturally after commas and full stops.

## Practice

1. Say **你好** three times slowly.
2. Say **我叫 ...** and add your own name.
3. Record or rehearse a full line: **你好，我叫 ...。很高兴认识你。**

## Answer Key or Rubric

Success looks like:

- phrases are understandable
- learner keeps the correct word order
- learner can complete a one-line self-introduction

If using self-review, ask:

- Could I say each phrase without stopping after every syllable?
- Did I keep **我叫** together as one chunk?

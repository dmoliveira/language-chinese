# Talk About Food Choices

## Objectives

- say what you like or want
- answer a simple food preference question

## Useful Speaking Chunks

- 我喜欢茶。— I like tea.
- 我想喝咖啡。— I would like coffee.
- 我要米饭。— I want rice.
- 你喜欢茶吗？— Do you like tea?

## Speaking Routine

1. say one drink you like
2. say one food you want
3. ask one simple question
4. answer it yourself

## Practice

- say 3 to 4 short lines about what you like or want to eat and drink
- then repeat with one different drink or food item

## Answer Key or Rubric

Success looks like:

- clear short phrases
- correct order of words
- one understandable question and answer

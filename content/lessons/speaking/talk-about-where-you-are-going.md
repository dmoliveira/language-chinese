# Talk About Where You Are Going

## Objectives

- say where you are going
- say when a simple plan happens

## Useful Speaking Chunks

- 我明天去学校。— I am going to school tomorrow.
- 我今天去图书馆。— I am going to the library today.
- 他来这里。— He is coming here.
- 我下午在学校。— I am at school in the afternoon.

## Speaking Routine

1. say the time
2. say where you are going
3. add one location detail
4. repeat the full short plan naturally

## Practice

- say 3 to 4 short lines about where you are going and when

## Answer Key or Rubric

Success looks like:

- clear time words
- a visible place word
- an understandable movement verb

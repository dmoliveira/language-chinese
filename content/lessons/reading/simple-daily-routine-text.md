# Simple Daily Routine Reading

## Objectives

- Read a short daily routine text.
- Recognize time expressions and routine verbs.
- Understand sequence in a short paragraph.

## Prerequisites

- Complete the starter reading lesson first.

## Lesson Content

> 我早上七点起床。<br>
> 我吃早饭。<br>
> 白天我学习。<br>
> 晚上我休息。<br>

## Practice

1. What time does the person get up?
2. Which word means evening?
3. What does the person do during the day?

## Answer Key or Rubric

1. Seven o’clock in the morning.
2. 晚上
3. Studies.

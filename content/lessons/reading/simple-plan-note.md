# Reading a Simple Plan Note

## Objectives

- read a short note about a place and time
- identify basic plan details

## Reading Text

- 我明天去学校。— I am going to school tomorrow.
- 下午我在图书馆。— In the afternoon I am at the library.

## What To Notice

- **明天** gives the time
- **去** gives movement
- **在图书馆** gives the location

## Practice

1. When does the person go to school?
2. Where is the person in the afternoon?
3. Which word means go?
4. Which word means library?

## Answer Key or Rubric

1. Tomorrow.
2. At the library.
3. **去**
4. **图书馆**

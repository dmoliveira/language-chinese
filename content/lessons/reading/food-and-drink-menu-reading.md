# Food and Drink Menu Reading

## Objectives

- read simple menu words
- understand basic food choices
- identify preferences and orders

## Before You Read

- Look first for drinks and foods you already recognize.
- Notice whether the line is a menu item, a question, or a choice.
- Do not try to translate everything word by word.

## Reading Text

- 茶 — tea
- 咖啡 — coffee
- 米饭 — rice
- 你要咖啡吗？— Do you want coffee?
- 我想喝茶。— I would like tea.

## What To Notice

- single-word items often work like menu labels
- questions often end with **吗**
- **想** can show a polite wish or preference

## Practice

1. Which word means tea?
2. Which line is a question?
3. What does the speaker want to drink?
4. Which item looks like a food and not a drink?
5. Read the menu items once without English support.

## Answer Key or Rubric

1. **茶**
2. **你要咖啡吗？**
3. **茶**
4. **米饭**
5. Success if the learner can roughly connect the visible menu items with meaning.

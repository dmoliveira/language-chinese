# Reading a Short Choice Explanation

## Objectives

- read a short connected explanation
- identify one choice and one reason

## Reading Text

- 因为我喜欢茶，所以我常常喝茶。

## What To Notice

- the sentence has a reason part and a result part
- because / so gives a clearer connection than two separate short sentences

## Practice

1. What does the speaker like?
2. What does the speaker often drink?
3. Which word means because?
4. Which word means so?

## Answer Key or Rubric

1. Tea.
2. Tea.
3. 因为
4. 所以

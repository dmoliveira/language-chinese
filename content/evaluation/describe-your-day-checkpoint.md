# Describe Your Day Checkpoint

## Purpose

Use this checkpoint to see whether you can understand and produce a short daily routine description.

## Task

- write or say 4 to 6 lines about your daily routine

## Success Criteria

- uses time words
- uses routine verbs
- has understandable sequence

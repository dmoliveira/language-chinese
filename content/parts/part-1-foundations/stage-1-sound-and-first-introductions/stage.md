# Stage 1 — Sound and First Introductions

## Purpose

Move from zero knowledge into a stable beginner foundation with pronunciation, first interactions, first reading, and first writing.

## Target Outcomes

- recognize core beginner phrases
- pronounce a few useful chunks clearly
- connect hanzi, pinyin, and meaning in a tiny text
- write a short introduction pattern

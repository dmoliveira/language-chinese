# Part I — Foundations

## Purpose

Build the strongest possible beginner base in pronunciation, pinyin, first sentence patterns, greetings, and first reading/writing.

## Learner Outcomes

- understand pinyin and tone basics
- produce a short self-introduction
- read a tiny supported text
- write a tiny supported self-introduction
- complete a first self-check and guided study plan

## Stage Sequence

- Stage 1 — Sound and First Introductions

## References and Review Support

- pronunciation and pinyin references
- starter vocabulary and sentence pattern references
- flashcards, quizzes, and the two-week guided plan

# Part III — Everyday Expansion

## Purpose

Help learners move from simple isolated beginner sentences into short connected everyday explanations.

## Learner Outcomes

- describe habits with more than one sentence
- compare simple preferences
- explain small everyday choices
- handle longer supported listening and reading passages

## Stage Sequence

- Stage 1 — Habits, Preferences, and Comparison
- Stage 2 — Reasons, Choices, and Short Explanations
- Stage 3 — Connected Everyday Descriptions
- Stage 4 — Opinions and Clarifications

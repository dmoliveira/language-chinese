# Two-Week Absolute Beginner Starter

## Audience

This plan is for learners who are starting Chinese from zero and want a short, structured beginning.

## Time Commitment

- 15 to 25 minutes per day
- 6 study days per week
- 1 lighter review or rest day per week

## Weekly Plan

### Week 1

- Day 1: read **Pinyin and Tone Basics** (`reference-foundations-001`)
- Day 2: study **Pronunciation, Tones, and Basic Self-Introduction** (`speaking-foundations-001`)
- Day 3: study **Greetings and Introductions** (`listening-foundations-001`)
- Day 4: review **Greetings and Introductions Deck** (`deck-foundations-001`)
- Day 5: study **First Characters and High-Frequency Words** (`reading-foundations-001`)
- Day 6: self-review and speak your introduction aloud

### Week 2

- Day 1: read **Pronunciation Guide** (`reference-foundations-002`)
- Day 2: study **Build Simple Self-Introduction Sentences** (`writing-foundations-001`)
- Day 3: review **Core Starter Vocabulary Deck** (`deck-foundations-002`)
- Day 4: repeat listening and speaking practice
- Day 5: do **Entry Self-Check** (`evaluation-foundations-001`)
- Day 6: do **Beginner Lesson Quiz** (`evaluation-foundations-002`)

## Review Checkpoints

- Can you say **你好，我叫 ...** clearly?
- Can you recognize **我**, **你**, and **好**?
- Can you read and understand a very short self-introduction?

## Completion Criteria

You complete this plan when you can:

- greet someone simply
- say your name in Chinese
- read a short beginner text with support
- complete the first self-check and lesson quiz pattern

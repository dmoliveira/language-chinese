# Eight-Week Beginner Foundation

## Audience

This plan is for learners who prefer slower pacing, more repetition, and steady confidence-building.

## Time Commitment

- 20 to 30 minutes per study day
- 5 study days per week
- extra optional review on weekends

## Weekly Plan

### Weeks 1 to 2

- Week 1: learn pinyin, tones, and pronunciation basics with **Pinyin and Tone Basics**
- Week 2: practice **Pronunciation, Tones, and Basic Self-Introduction** every study day

### Weeks 3 to 4

- Week 3: study **Greetings and Introductions** and repeat it across two or three sessions
- Week 4: review **Greetings and Introductions Deck** and repeat beginner chunks aloud

### Weeks 5 to 6

- Week 5: study **First Characters and High-Frequency Words**
- Week 6: review **Core Starter Vocabulary Deck** and practice recognition of key words and characters

### Weeks 7 to 8

- Week 7: study **Build Simple Self-Introduction Sentences**
- Week 8: complete **Entry Self-Check**, **Beginner Lesson Quiz**, and **Beginner Milestone Checklist**

## Review Checkpoints

- Can you hear and repeat key beginner phrases?
- Can you read the first short text with support?
- Can you write a short self-introduction?
- Can you explain your current strengths and gaps?

## Completion Criteria

You complete this plan when you can:

- understand and produce the core starter phrases
- read the first guided text with meaning support
- write a short self-introduction
- finish the beginner milestone checklist
